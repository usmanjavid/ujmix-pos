# Customer activation policy

New installations have a full seven-day trial from first use. After it ends, new billing needs an activated license. Saved records, invoice viewing/printing and backups remain accessible.

Available terms: 3 months, 6 months, 1 year, 3 years and lifetime. The signed license identifies the registered shop/customer and, for newly issued licenses, the PC activation code. Terms begin at issuance, use UTC calendar months and clamp month ends. Lifetime means no license expiry date; it does not promise indefinite support, free upgrades or uninterrupted third-party services.

After an activated term expires, seven days of checkout grace apply. Once grace ends, new POS/manual invoices require renewal. Existing records, invoice viewing/printing, backups and activation remain accessible. Expired license files cannot be newly activated. Offline checks depend on the PC clock.

Contact the owner for replacement-PC transfer. Transfer preserves the original expiry, rather than restarting a paid term. Deactivate/remove the old installation first; an offline old copy cannot be remotely revoked. Existing unbound licenses remain compatible. Keep the customer license file and purchase record secure.

Customer licenses do not transfer ownership of the software or authorize redistribution of the owner generator/issuer key. See the application LICENSE for legal terms; clarify commercial support and upgrade coverage with the seller before purchase.
